Model and methodology

Model

The selected algorithm groups a generated two-dimensional dataset according to its own distance, density or connectivity criterion. The step controls expose intermediate assignments; the algorithm-specific explanation describes the current procedure.

Assumptions and limitations

These synthetic datasets illustrate algorithm behaviour. A cluster is not necessarily a natural category: its shape and membership depend on the metric, parameters, initialisation and data geometry. Compare algorithms on the same dataset and inspect failed as well as successful partitions. Displayed metrics summarise the chosen criterion; they do not establish scientific validity.
